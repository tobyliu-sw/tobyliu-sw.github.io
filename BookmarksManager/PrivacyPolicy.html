Information Collection and Use

Bookmarks Manager is designed with privacy as a priority. I do not collect, transmit, or store any of your personal data on external servers.

All data processing, including bookmark organization, sorting, duplicate detection ("Find Similar"), and broken link validation ("Find Broken"), happens locally on your device within your browser.

Data Access and Permissions

To function correctly, Bookmarks Manager requires specific permissions:

  • Bookmarks: The core permission used to read, modify, organize, and delete bookmarks at your request. This data remains strictly local to your browser.
  • Tabs & ActiveTab: Used to open bookmarks in new tabs and to capture the title/URL of the current page when you use the "Add Bookmark" popup.
  • Web Navigation: Used exclusively by the "Find Broken Bookmarks" feature to detect navigation success or failure when validating local `file://` links in a background tab.
  • Host Permissions (`http://*/*`, `https://*/*`): Required for the "Find Broken Bookmarks" feature to send `HEAD` or `GET` requests to verify if external links are valid. These requests do not transmit user data.

Log Data

I want to inform you that whenever you use my Service, in a case of an error in the extension I do not collect any Log Data or crash reports remotely. Any errors logged are local to your browser's developer console.

Cookies

This Service does not use "cookies". It does not track your browsing history outside of the specific interactions required to validate bookmark links or add the current page.
